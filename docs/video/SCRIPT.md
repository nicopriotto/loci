# LOCI demo video: guion v3

Reglas: narracion continua, ritmo rapido, una sola voz (ElevenLabs, voz Brian), subtitulos karaoke abajo todo el tiempo.
Tres trucos numerados: 1 palacio de la memoria, 2 lo absurdo (al inicio), 3 repeticion espaciada (en medio de la demo).
Duracion objetivo: 2:10 a 2:40. Requisito Meta: menos de 3:00, footage del emulador, lo mejor primero.

Leyenda: [R] Remotion, [VR] captura del emulador, [S] Studio web.

---

## 1. cold — Cold open [VR paneo lento por el living con las 8 cards ya puestas, sin tocar ninguna]
This room is a memory palace. Every card on these walls is something you will never forget. Just look around, and it all comes back.

## 2. problem — El problema [R curva del olvido]
Here's the problem. You read something today, and by next week, most of it is gone. It's called the forgetting curve, and we all have one.

## 3. palace — Truco 1: palacio de la memoria [R casa; Milk en la lampara, Eggs en la pared, Bread en el sofa]
Luckily, there are tricks. Trick number one: the memory palace. Picture a place you know, like your living room, and leave each thing you want to remember in a spot. Milk on the lamp. Eggs on the wall. Bread on the sofa. Later, you just walk through the room, and everything is right where you left it.

## 4. bizarre — Truco 2: lo absurdo [R pala clara, luego elefante violeta paleando]
Trick number two: make it weird. Your brain loves weird. A shovel on a table? Forgettable. A purple elephant shoveling on the table? You'll remember that for weeks. Psychologists call it the bizarreness effect.

## 5. turn — El giro [R casa se llena de color; corte directo a la web, sin clip VR]
The catch? Both tricks are hard. You have to build the palace in your head, and invent every strange picture yourself. So we built LOCI. In VR, you don't imagine the palace. You walk into it.

## 6. studio — Web [S sin zoom: PDF, escribir palabras, imagen con Meta AI, subir imagen, elegir cuarto]
It starts on the web. Drop in a PDF or paste your notes, and LOCI reads them and pulls out the key ideas. Prefer your own? Just type the words you want to remember. Then give each idea a picture. Describe it, and Meta's AI draws it for you. Or let it draw every card at once, each one a little stranger than the last. Got a picture of your own? Just upload it. Then pick a room: a sunlit living room, a kitchen, a bedroom, or your own space. And build your palace.

## 7. enter — Entrar [VR abanico de 8 cards]
Put on your Quest, and all your cards are waiting right in front of you.

## 8. place — Colocar [VR primera card a 1x, las otras 7 aceleradas, continuo]
Pinch a card and place it anywhere in the room. On the wall, next to the lamp, by the window. One after another, every idea gets a home, and a dotted path keeps them in order.

## 9. review — Repaso [R placa "Two days later" + VR repaso desde la card 1, las 8 visibles]
Two days later, you come back and walk the path, starting with card one. Look at a card, try to recall it, then pinch to flip it. Got it? Great. Missed it? No problem.

## 10. spaced — Truco 3: repeticion espaciada [R diagrama]
That's trick number three: spaced repetition. Cards you remember come back later. Cards you miss come back sooner. So LOCI always knows which ideas need a little more work today.

## 11. done — Panel final [VR panel "Palace reviewed" capturado de cerca, sin zoom digital]
One look around the room, and you know exactly what you know.

## 12. close — Cierre [VR segundo paneo con las cards puestas + R placa final]
LOCI runs right in the browser on Meta Quest, built with the Immersive Web SDK, in your own room or in one of ours.
Stop memorizing. Start remembering.
